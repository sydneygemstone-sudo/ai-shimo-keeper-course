Ch.1 · The Egg · 90 min (incl. 10' break)

Hatching Day

Trial . Hatch . Name it

A glowing egg and a recruitment letter turn up in class. An AI familiar sleeps inside. Only a class that clears three Trials wakes it.

Badge: Keen EyeEnglish: AI · pattern · learn
Teacher's Hidden Card · read alone. Never project.
  • Real goals. ① Today's AI = a program that sees many examples and finds its own patterns. Man-made tool. Not magic, not alive. ② It senses through sensors: what it never saw or learned, it can only guess -- never fake knowing. ③ Narrow examples grow fake rules. Only new samples catch them (core activity: The Plate Secret, the six-step loop on paper).
  • Paper simulation. Fluff answers by one hidden rule in the deck. It drills the order of an experiment -- no live model is trained here (that is Ch.2). Gates: no challenge until trained, no advancing until done. Backdoor = 🔓 on the challenge page.
  • Must know AI / pattern / learn; heard once: sensor. Prep: iPads bookmarked to autodraw.com + quickdraw.withgoogle.com (try both yourself), opaque lens cap, dimmable lights.
  • Demo discipline, all 5 chapters. It works -> observe, name, explain. It fails -> say so plainly, ask "why this time", switch to the recording. Never fake what the screen did not show.

The glowing egg and the letter

🎭 Story scene
Lights half down. No intro. No "today's lesson." Drop your voice and open cold.
T
Shh. Not a sound. You see the egg too, right? It wasn't here last night. This morning it was lying there, glowing -- with a letter under it.
Each kid says or draws one guess: what is AI? Write it down word for word. Say: "No right answer yet. After it hatches we check if you levelled up."
To the whole class:

20-33'Trial 1

Puppet & familiar (unplugged)

🅰 Puppet round

Take the cue card. Follow it exactly. No variation. Then give a signal that isn't on it: two taps, left shoulder. The puppet checks and answers "Not on the card."

🅱 Familiar round

No card. Watch each move demoed twice, then copy from the examples. End on that same never-shown signal.

Left shoulder -> right hand ✋ · Right shoulder -> stand 🧍
Head -> spin 🔄 · High-five -> squat ⬇️
Hidden test: two taps, left shoulder = ??? Nobody taught it.
T
All three outcomes are fine. Wrong: "Examples mean guessing wrong sometimes." Right but unsure: "You guessed. So does it." All right: "Sharp eyes -- no promise you never slip." Then the new signal: no example means guess, or say "never seen it". Trial 1 passed!
Same kid does both rounds: you call from the card, then demo each move twice. Both end on two taps, left shoulder.
33-40'Codex Study ②

Magic Eye and Magic Ear

T
A familiar has no eyes and no ears. It uses a Magic Eye (camera) and a Magic Ear (mic). The Codex calls both sensor.
Cap the iPad camera. Ask: "Still see cloth or shadow?" Land it: not "nothing" -- the camera is just getting very little. We covered the Eye only; the Ear may still listen. Link: why won't the lift door pinch you?
What it was not shown, it can only guess.You decide what it sees and hears. Big power, big responsibility.
Tamer rule one: Magic Eye and Magic Ear -- ask an adult first, switch off after.

50-64'Trial 2

The Plate Secret: Feed 10 Memories

🗒 Paper sim. Fluff answers by one hidden rule
T
Say: "Fluff's first skill is knowing fruit. These are training cards, not a test. Name the food, flip the label, it's fed." 10 fed -> digest -> the gate opens.
Training book 0 / 10

Teacher only. Do not project. The cards hide a plate shortcut: fruit on red, non-fruit on blue. Do NOT reveal it. Child spots it early: "You found what the cards share. Don't call it Fluff's rule yet. Wait for evidence." Child mislabels: "Flip the real label. Fluff is fed checked labels only."
Challenge Gate

Banana on a Blue Plate

🗒 One step at a time. Lock the prediction before the reveal
